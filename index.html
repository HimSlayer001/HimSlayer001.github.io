01 / ABOUTAtom | Byte | Gene

I'm a developer based in Hyderabad who likes to understand things from the atom up: how a shell parses a command, how a retriever ranks a document, how a seizure shows up in an EEG trace. My main focus is AI agents, and I'm going deeper into systems programming, distributed systems and search, and I care about measuring what I build, because a system I can't evaluate is one I can't trust.
